4. Organization & Management

The company will be legally structured as a Texas Limited Liability Company (LLC), wholly and equitably owned by its founding partners. Management execution is clearly segregated by executive operational expertise and head coaching/programming credentials.

Core Leadership Team

  • Patty Land (Co-Owner / Director of Operations): Manages corporate financial health, regulatory compliance, commercial lease negotiations, technology architecture, B2B wellness network expansion, and business development initiatives.
  • Daveyanna Garcia (Co-Owner / Head of Programming & Coaching): Manages technical program design, physical class execution, standard operating procedures for member safety and retention, and recruitment/oversight of tactical coaching personnel.

Staffing & Labor Architecture

To preserve lean operational overhead through the critical growth phases, the facility will intentionally sidestep heavy fixed-payroll burdens. Group classes outside the scope of the founding partners will be serviced by certified 1099 independent contractor fitness professionals, paid on an optimized, per-class flat-rate model.

7. Operational & Facility Plan

Target Real Estate Parameters

We are targeting an industrial warehouse configuration comprising approximately 3,500 square feet along the North Central San Antonio / Hwy 281 corridor. This size represents the operational sweet spot for our model, allowing a highly efficient square-footage utilization strategy:

  • Primary Workout Floor (~2,000 sq ft): Engineered to support an athlete density ratio of 125 sq ft per individual, comfortably scaling up to our hard class cap of 15 simultaneous participants safely utilizing barbells and dynamic movements.
  • Rig & Equipment Perimeter (~600 sq ft): Allocates structured space for wall-mounted heavy-duty pull-up systems and storage trees for rows, bikes, and free weights.
  • Integrated "Wellness Hub" Footprint (~300 sq ft): A dedicated, enclosed clinical or physical therapy partitioned workspace allocated for complementary B2B sub-leasing opportunities.
  • Administrative & Logistics (~600 sq ft): Dedicated space for member onboarding reception, individual storage cubbies, and dual single-occupancy commercial restrooms.
Location Selection Controls: Selection criteria strictly mandate verifying light industrial or commercial recreational zoning allowances, verifying an abundance of flexible early-morning/late-evening communal parking spaces, and ensuring structurally sound slab flooring capable of handling repetitive, high-impact barbell weights.

8. Financial Plan: Source & Use of Funds

The total estimated capitalization required to successfully deploy the facility is $160,000. This project will be capitalized via a 10% cash equity injection ($16,000) from Co-Owner Patty Land, alongside a requested $144,000 SBA 7(a) small business loan structure.

Category Allocation Strategic & Operational Purpose
Lease Contingency & Deposit $7,000 Secures target 3,500 sq ft industrial facility. Capital covers standard first month base rent and commercial security deposit optimized at an annual lease benchmark of ≤$12/sq ft.
Tenant Improvements (TI) $50,000 Transforms open industrial layout to commercial compliance. Outlays include high-impact seamless vulcanized rubber athletic flooring, lighting upgrades, high-velocity cooling systems, and specialized plumbing fixtures.
Commercial Equipment Package $60,000 Procurement of professional commercial structural rigs, Olympic-grade steel barbells, bumper weight packages, dumbbells, conditioning machinery, and auxiliary mobility gear.
Technology, Legal, & Branding $8,000 Covers state LLC filings, formal partnership agreements, comprehensive facility liability insurance deposits, administrative client software integration, and structural exterior signage.
Working Capital Reserve $35,000 Strategic liquidity buffer held explicitly to preserve 6+ months of uninterrupted operational capacity covering fixed utilities, marketing outreach, and coaching resources as membership scales.
TOTAL: $160,000 Total Capital Capitalization Blueprint

Capital Distribution Controls & Guarantees

  • Founder's Debt Instrument: The $16,000 seed cash will be structured legally as a Priority Founder's Loan in the LLC Operating Agreement, returning principal capital to the investor with fixed interest prior to traditional equity profit distributions.
  • Personal Guarantees: To align interests and assure financial compliance with institutional lending guidelines, both co-owners will act as joint personal guarantors on the underlying SBA loan vehicle.
  • Sub-Lease Revenue Optimization: Operating risk is heavily offset by the implementation of our "Wellness Hub" sub-leasing model. Budgeting an estimated $750/month passive lease return from a physical therapy or adjacent health provider directly absorbs approximately 21% of our entire structural lease overhead from inception.
